# David Guo

I’m a MASc student in the [Data-Driven Decision Making (D3M) Lab](https://d3m.mie.utoronto.ca/) at the University of Toronto, supervised by Prof. Scott Sanner, working on machine learning for interactive AI systems.

My work spans **conversational recommendation**, **user modeling**, and **memory for Agentic systems**, combining empirical studies of interaction with building and evaluating systems.

### Publications & Preprints

- **[Have I Scene This Before?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05526)** — Spatially grounded conversational memory for egocentric assistants. Introduces the Ego-SpaCR benchmark and SpaC-MEM object-centric memory.
- **[VOGUE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21151)** · ACM UMAP 2026 — A human–human conversational recommendation dataset, with analyses of interaction and multimodal language-model preference inference.
- **[Scene-based In-Situ Item Labeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09698)** · Findings of ACL 2026 — Evaluating which information immersive recommendation systems should present to users.
